What Google allows, in plain terms.

The Places API returns at most five reviews per place, and only the ones Google chooses. That is a hard limit on their side; no setting here changes it.

Review text may not be stored permanently, so it lives in a short cache that expires on the TTL below and is purged on every sync. The only thing kept for good is the Place ID.

The reviews field is billed on the Enterprise + Atmosphere tier, so leave the TTL generous rather than fetching on every page view. For the permanent gallery, upload your own photographs instead of relying on Place photos.
